# AST Compass Brand

Canonical name: **AST Compass**

Primary domain: **astcompass.com**

Primary logo: the canonical culture-plate compass mark in `src/assets/brand/ast-compass-icon.svg`, paired with the AST Compass wordmark where space permits.

## Logo philosophy

A compass representing guidance through organisms, resistance mechanisms, phenotype, and AST interpretation. The culture-plate ring and inhibition-zone details connect the mark to microbiology while the needle conveys direction without implying automation.

## Taglines

- Think beyond S and R.
- From organism to mechanism to interpretation.
- A compass, not an autopilot.

## Usage

Use the same canonical logo everywhere. Preserve clear space equal to at least one quarter of the icon diameter. Scale proportionally and use the icon-only variant only where the full lockup will not fit.

## Do not

- Redraw the compass differently.
- Change the needle geometry.
- Use alternate icons.
- Stretch the logo or change its proportions.
- Combine the logo with unrelated imagery.

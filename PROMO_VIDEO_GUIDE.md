# AST Compass Promo Video Guide

## How to record the AST Compass ad

1. Open `https://astcompass.com/promo` after the promo route is deployed.
2. Use **Chrome desktop responsive mode** and select a 9:16 viewport, ideally 1080 × 1920. Desktop recording gives the most consistent frame, cursor control, and clean audio workflow. An iPhone or Android screen recording also works, but device chrome and notifications are harder to control.
3. Hide bookmarks and unrelated tabs. Turn off notifications.
4. Start the screen recorder.
5. Press **Full Screen**, then press **Start Demo** before recording the final take. If full-screen mode hides the controls on your browser, press **Start Demo** first and immediately press **Full Screen**.
6. Let the full seven-scene sequence run. It lasts about 25.5 seconds and resets to the brand scene afterward.
7. Stop recording after the final CTA.
8. Trim the beginning and end in CapCut.
9. Export at 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264, with high quality/bitrate.

## CapCut edit plan

1. Create a 9:16 project and import the screen recording and voice-over.
2. Trim dead time before Start Demo and after the final CTA.
3. Align the first spoken word with the opening brand scene.
4. Use short text overlays only where they reinforce the scene:
   - See the mechanism behind the result.
   - Explore BCID resistance patterns.
   - Connect genes with phenotype.
   - Check concordance.
   - Learn AST through cases.
   - Visit astcompass.com.
5. Keep overlays inside the center safe area so social-app controls do not cover them.
6. Use simple fade or dissolve transitions; avoid flashy effects.
7. Add subtle instrumental music at roughly 8–12% volume under narration. Keep narration clear and do not use music with prominent vocals.
8. Generate captions, correct scientific terms manually, and verify: AST, BCID, CTX-M, KPC, phenotype, and astcompass.com.
9. Keep the final `ASTCOMPASS.COM` frame visible for at least two seconds.
10. Export 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC. Watch the exported file once with sound and once muted before posting.

## Claims boundary

The promo must remain educational. Do not add claims such as clinically validated, FDA approved, best therapy, treat faster, or improved patient outcomes.

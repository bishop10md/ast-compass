## ADF-001 — MIC direction

- Topic: AST Fundamentals
- Difficulty: Foundation
- Type: multiple-choice
- Learning objective: Explain mic direction in an AST reasoning workflow.

**Question:** Which statement best describes an MIC?

A. The lowest tested concentration preventing visible growth
B. The diameter of inhibition around a disk
C. A molecular resistance-marker count
D. A treatment dose

**Correct answer:** The lowest tested concentration preventing visible growth

**Explanation:** An MIC is the lowest tested antimicrobial concentration that prevents visible growth under the method conditions.

**Teaching point:** A measured MIC is not itself a clinical category or dose.

**Sources:** ref-clsi
